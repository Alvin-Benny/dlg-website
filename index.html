01 — About

A disciplined approach to property and capital.

Drawing upon 50 years of combined professional experience, DLG brings together commercial expertise, strategic capital management and a disciplined approach to project execution.

Our strategy is centred on identifying underutilised assets, unlocking development potential and delivering commercially viable projects across a diversified property portfolio.

As a privately held organisation, we maintain the agility, autonomy and commercial flexibility to act on emerging market opportunities — while building enduring relationships with capital partners, landowners, industry professionals and other key stakeholders.

We believe sustainable wealth is created through disciplined decision-making, purposeful development and responsible stewardship of capital. Our ambition extends beyond individual projects: to build an enduring enterprise recognised for integrity, execution capability and value creation across market cycles.

Our vision

To establish DLG as a respected privately owned property development enterprise, creating enduring assets, sustainable communities and long-term commercial value.

Our mission

To identify, acquire and develop strategically positioned property assets through rigorous commercial assessment, disciplined capital allocation and effective project delivery.

02 — Focus areas

Where we create value.

The Group is building its capability across four complementary areas of the property sector.

i.

Residential development

Townhouses, duplexes, apartments and medium-density residential developments in strategically selected growth corridors.

ii.

Land acquisition & subdivision

Strategic site acquisitions, land subdivisions, planning uplift and development-ready land opportunities.

iii.

Specialised accommodation

Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA) and other residential assets addressing identifiable housing demand.

iv.

Strategic property investments

Selective investments, joint ventures and asset repositioning opportunities with identifiable value-creation potential.

03 — Approach

From opportunity to realisation.

A six-stage development methodology that applies the same rigour at every step of a project's life.

  1. 01

    Opportunity origination

    Site identification, market research and acquisition negotiations.

  2. 02

    Commercial due diligence

    Feasibility, planning assessment, financial modelling and risk analysis.

  3. 03

    Capital structuring

    Equity allocation, debt financing and project-specific funding.

  4. 04

    Planning & approvals

    Design optimisation, consultants and statutory approvals.

  5. 05

    Project execution

    Construction, procurement, cost management and delivery oversight.

  6. 06

    Asset realisation

    Strategic disposal, refinancing or long-term asset retention.
